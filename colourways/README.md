this is my only light theme. i started with actual spectra of the "heart
pluto" image from 2015, and then used that as the basis for the theme. i then
more or less inverted it to give myself a darker complement to it with a
similar palette because i just wanted it. truthfully i don't use the light one
very often because i find it kind of searing. the dark theme, i like a lot,
although it's on the limit of what i can see comfortably.

i find these pastel themes are actually great for clarity and legibility. it
seems like it might be kind of a lot to have this bright pink terminal, but
the legibility, particularly of log files and large text documents is just
really hard to argue with. the invert version is used when i have my screen
inverted. having your screen inverted can be *really* helpful when your eyes
are not cooperating, and it's convenient to then have a theme that sets your
editor and markdown viewer and so on to be correct when your screen is
inverted.

this is another theme i like a lot for its legibility and the fact that it has
some amount of color in it, although it is pretty much past what i can use
comfortably today. this was taken directly from x-ray spectra. this was a
design goal for me in the last set of additions to `libtheme` -- i was telling
my friend, *"you know, i've got this color library, and i've got this ray
tracing thing, and color is just light, and light is just radiation, and i
derived that color library from first principles, so there's no reason i
couldn't just have gamma ray bursts cast into visible light as themes for
`zsh`."* and now i have it. i'm very pleased with this.

## neptune-voyager

<table>
<tr>
<td align="center">neptune-voyager<br>
<img src="pictures/neptune-voyager.png" width="290"
alt="neptune-voyager: pale blue ink on a dark grey ground"></td>
</tr>
</table>

another dark blue theme, same as the other ones: i can't really make out light
on black anymore, so the way to get a "dark" theme for me is to bring the
black up, take the chroma and lum down, and you wind up with like a greyish on
blueish kind of colourway. this, like pluto and fermi, was adjusted into
visible light, and then into css and colors for the terminal, from imagery of
neptune. it's not "like" the planet, this is actually light that came from the
planet. and now it's on your computer. *math,* man.
